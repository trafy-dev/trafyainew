Zero to one

How you go from zero

Every founder starts this cohort with zero followers and zero users. Growth isn't a module you sit through in Month 4, it's a discipline that runs underneath every month of the curriculum.

  1. 01

    Build in public from day one

    Post progress daily, not just the finished product. An audience that watches you build trusts you more than one that only sees the launch.

  2. 02

    Talk to 10 users before you write a feature

    Validation comes before code. Every feature should trace back to a conversation with a real, named user.

  3. 03

    One channel, mastered

    Pick a single platform, LinkedIn, X, Instagram or YouTube, and go deep instead of spreading thin.

  4. 04

    Ship weekly, not monthly

    Small, compounding releases beat one big launch. Momentum is a growth channel of its own.

  5. 05

    Let the cohort be your first distribution

    150+ builders in the room amplifying each other's work is real distribution, use it before you look anywhere else.
