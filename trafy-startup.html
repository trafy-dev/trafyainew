The journey

A proper series of rounds, not a one-shot pitch

Three phases, nine checkpoints. Every idea that makes it through has already survived a real room.

Phase 1 · Validate
  1. IdeationYou bring the idea. We help you shape it.
  2. Ideation ReviewPitch it to Trafy CTOs & investors, and get real feedback, not a rubric.
  3. Idea EvaluationMarket potential, problem fit, team and feasibility, checked properly before anyone moves forward.
Idea passed → straight into Phase 2 Not a fit yet → we share feedback and resources, and you can reapply anytime
Phase 2 · Build
  1. C-Suite MentorshipPaired with a CEO, CTO, CMO or CFO mentor who's built and scaled before, working with you directly.
  2. Build & ValidateBuild your MVP with expert guidance, tools and technical support behind you.
  3. Prototype MilestoneShip a working prototype, and you unlock the next level of Trafy incubation.
Phase 3 · Fund
  1. Networking OpportunitiesAccess to Trafy's founder network, workshops, events and a peer community that's building too.
  2. Pre-Seeding SupportHelp with your pitch deck, investor intros, and actually raising your pre-seed round.
  3. VC MeetingsMeet and pitch Trafy's network of VCs and angels directly. We open doors, you close rounds.
